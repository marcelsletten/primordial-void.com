A fresh batch of re-presses from Shed’s sought-after Wax series has just landed in our distro shop. Today, we have five titles available in limited quantities: No. 30003 (2010), No. 70007 (2019), No. 80008 (2022), No. 90009 (2024), and last April’s No. 11110 (2026). For well over a decade now, this project of German producer René Pawlowitz has been responsible for some of the greatest dub and minimal tech cuts in recent memory. Considering these discs are rather difficult to find over here in the States, it only made sense for us to grab some of our very favorites in the series for our store.
