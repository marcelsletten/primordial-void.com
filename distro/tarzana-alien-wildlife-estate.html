PV is honored to be handling exclusive USA distro for a bundle of new Spencer Clark (Skaters, Monopoly Child Star Searchers, Monopoly Child Star Searchers, etc.) cassette reissues. Next up is the first-ever tape edition of Tarzana’s Alien Wildlife Estate, a brilliant 2015 collaboration between Clark and Finnish artist Jan Anderzen. Deep, psychedelic exotica zones on this one. The dynamic duo of visionaries Clark and Anderzen would go on to produce more Tarzana music following the release of Alien Wildlife Estate (including a split album with Sun Araw!), but this one holds a special place in our hearts. The most fully realized example of the pair’s quest for producing uncanny ambient hymns for outer space cryptids.
