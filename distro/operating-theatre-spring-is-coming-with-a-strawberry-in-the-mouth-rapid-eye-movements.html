You already know this is a huge release for us to stock. We shouldn’t have to explain how important these collected works are to PV, but here’s a primer: we discovered the mind-bending, prescient recordings of Irish electronic pop genius Roger Doyle and his experimental theatre group Operating Theatre back in 2021, around the time we moved the label to Athens, Georgia, and long before a certain alt-pop star made “Spring Is Coming With a Strawberry in the Mouth” popular among the Instagram hipster crowd. We had never heard anything quite like it, and thanks to Allchival’s reissue of the 2xCD compilation The Early Years, these Fairlight-heavy bits of peak ‘80s synthpop were introduced to a new generation of experimental electronic connoisseurs. Cuts like “Queen of No Heart,” “Part of My Make-up,” and the eternal masterpiece “Spring Is Coming” absolutely predicted the eerie hauntology of OPN and the hyperreal pop of outfits like Hype Williams around the start of the 2010s. Pretty much everyone involved with PV back then was obsessed with these works by Doyle, performer Olwen Fouéré, and vocalist Elena López, and a separate 2023 2xLP comp paired the biggest fan favorites from The Early Years with the B-side from Operating Theatre’s 1981 debut, “Rapid Eye Movements.”

Today, we are pleased to be distributing some stateside copies of the aforementioned ‘23 comp, entitled Spring Is Coming With a Strawberry in the Mouth / Rapid Eye Movements, recently re-pressed with new artwork and a bonus 7” single (!) of “Spring Is Coming” and its instrumental version. It’s a beautiful package, and well worth the price of admission. Also exclusive to this compilation is Wah Wah Wino co-founder Morgan Buckley’s brilliant MIDI remix of “Spring Is Coming.” A must-have for any collection!
